Thesis

Claude's reading of the numbers
  • A membership business with very high returns. Return on invested capital was 43.6% in FY26 on a gross margin of only 5.4%. Membership fees rose 11% to $5.9B, paid members grew to 84.1M from 81.0M, and the renewal rate was 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada.10-K FY26
  • Growth is speeding up, not slowing down. Net sales rose 10.1% to $297.2B in FY26 and 11.2% in Q4. Revenue growth sped up by 3.6 points over three quarters, and Costco beat consensus EPS in each of the last four quarters.8-K 24 Sep 2026
  • The price already assumes much more than the business delivers. At 40.5x next year's earnings, a 2.1% free cash flow yield and a PEG of 4.07, the price implies growth about 9 points higher than Costco's own record. Consensus expects about 8% revenue growth and 9.9% EPS growth.Scorecard

Forward growth · named drivers

Pillar 3 · score 52
  • Comparable sales.Comparable sales rose 8% in FY26 (7% excluding gasoline and currency), from a 5% rise in average ticket and a 3% rise in shopping frequency.10-K FY26
  • New warehouses.Costco opened 28 new warehouses in FY26 (25 net new) and plans to open up to 33, including five relocations, in FY27. It operates 939 in total.10-K FY26
  • Executive upgrades and fees.Executive members grew to 42.3M from 38.7M and account for about 75.2% of net sales. Fee revenue rose 11% on sign-ups, the fee increase and upgrades.10-K FY26
  • Digital sales.Digitally-enabled comparable sales grew 21% in FY26 and 19.5% in Q4.8-K 24 Sep 2026
