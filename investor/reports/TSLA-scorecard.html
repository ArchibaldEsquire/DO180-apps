Thesis

Claude's reading of the numbers
  • The car business has stalled. Q3 2026 revenue was $28.0B, flat on a year earlier, and deliveries fell 2% to 486,532. GAAP operating income fell to $718M from $1,624M, as operating expenses rose to $4.8B from $3.4B.8-K 21 Oct 2026
  • The story is autonomy, software and energy. Active FSD subscriptions rose to 1.74M from 1.04M a year earlier. Cybercab Robotaxi rides started in Austin on 3 September, and Megafactory Texas began production with 50 GWh of planned capacity.8-K 21 Oct 2026
  • The price assumes that story comes true on a large scale. At 185x next year's earnings and a 0.2% free cash flow yield, the price implies growth about 53 points a year above Tesla's own record. Even the 5-year median P/E is 137.6x, and the current multiple is 3.2 times that.Scorecard
